# Spectral delay filters

DAFx-09, Como, September 1–4, 2009. Focused review of sections 2–4.
[Source](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2009/papers/paper_36.pdf).
The authors describe first-order allpass cascades, a chirp-envelope equalizer,
delayed feedback and coefficient variation. Equalization modifies magnitude;
feedback stability must account for loop gain including that equalizer.
Their [companion page](https://research.spa.aalto.fi/publications/papers/dafx09-sdf/)
provides audio examples, which are not acceptance evidence for this project.

The [local method](../methods/frequency_dispersion.md) omits envelope EQ and outer
feedback. Its normalized lattice realization, coefficient bound, controls and
engineering budgets are local derivations, not paper benchmark claims.
No complete reproduction of the paper's experimental results is claimed.
